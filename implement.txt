The backend needs a COMPLETE rewrite because:

❌ Current backend is for a delivery app
   - Only handles 6 service types
   - Simple booking flow
   - No provider/artisan system
   - No check-in/out
   - No violation system
   - No escrow payments

✅ New backend will be for a SERVICE MARKETPLACE
   - Dynamic service categories (plumbing, electrical, carpentry, etc.)
   - Provider/artisan registration and verification
   - Job posting and bidding system
   - Check-in/out with GPS + Photos + OTP
   - Violation and accountability system
   - Escrow payments with OPay/Paystack/Flutterwave
   - Provider and customer ratings
   - Account deletion with clean record check
